常见问题

需要注册账号吗？
不需要。打开就能用，所有功能都不需要登录。
换了手机，作品会丢吗？
不会。作品通过你自己的 iCloud 在设备间同步，只要两台设备登录同一个 Apple ID 就会自动恢复。可以在「设置 → Apple ID → iCloud」里查看或关闭本 App 的 iCloud 权限。
买过的通行证换设备后怎么恢复？
进入「我的 → 商店 → 恢复购买」，用原来的 Apple ID 即可恢复，不会重复扣费。
图纸上的色号能直接买到豆子吗？
可以。导出的高清图纸会印上对应品牌的真实商品编号，目前支持 Perler、Hama、Artkal、咪小窝（MARD）。在「设置 → 色卡」里切换品牌，已有作品会自动换算到最接近的颜色。
我手里的豆子颜色不齐怎么办？
在色卡设置里打开「我有的颜色」，勾选你实际拥有的色号，之后生成的图纸只会用这些颜色。
照片转出来的图纸不好看？
试试这几点：选主体清晰、背景干净的照片；适当调高格子数；在编辑界面手动修掉零散的杂色。
怎么删除我的数据？
「我的 → 设置 → 清空所有数据」会删除本机全部内容；卸载 App 也会一并删除。

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?
No. Everything works straight away — there is no sign-up and no login.
Will I lose my work if I change phone?
No. Your work syncs through your own iCloud. Sign in to the same Apple ID on the new device and it restores automatically. You can review or disable this under Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.
How do I restore my Bead Pass on a new device?
Go to Me → Shop → Restore Purchases and sign in with the Apple ID you bought it with. You will not be charged twice.
Can I actually order the beads listed on a chart?
Yes. Exported high-resolution charts print the real product codes for your chosen brand — Perler, Hama, Artkal and MARD are supported. Switch brands under Settings → Palette; your saved work is converted to the closest matching shades.
I only own part of a colour set.
Turn on “Colours I own” in the palette settings and tick the codes you actually have. Every chart will then be generated using only those.
My photo did not convert well.
Try a photo with a clear subject and clean background, raise the grid size, and clean up stray pixels by hand in the editor.
How do I delete my data?
Me → Settings → Clear all data removes everything on the device. Deleting the app also removes it.
